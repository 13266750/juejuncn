About Juejun

We put great content and great products on equal footing.

Based in Xiamen, Xiamen Juejun Culture Communication Co., Ltd. is a TikTok-first cross-border e-commerce and cultural communication company. We believe cross-border business is not about moving goods, but about telling the world the craftsmanship and quality of Chinese manufacturing through authentic, relatable content.

From sourcing and creative content to creator collaboration, fulfillment, and review, we let data drive every decision and operate each market with a long-term mindset—so global consumers can easily discover and fall for Chinese brands.

Mission
Use short-video and livestream content to break cultural barriers and help global consumers discover and love Chinese brands.
Vision
Become a trusted bridge connecting Chinese manufacturing with young global consumers.
Authentic Content
No exaggeration or tricks—build trust through real-use scenarios.
Compliant Operations
Strictly follow platform policies and category certifications for steady, sustainable global expansion.
